# Unified recording — 19 October 2026

Implements Release 1 of the supplied 18 October usability and recording brief, retaining the five familiar tabs, password-first authentication, existing URLs, currencies, original transaction IDs/dates, isolated demo and exact minor-unit arithmetic. Release 2 recurrence/reminders and Release 3 accounts/transfers/valuations are deliberately outside this release. “Accounts” in the brief means financial accounts, not removal of the existing private sign-in requirement.

## Calculation gate and decisions

The supplied contracts reconcile with totals, moneyPlanSummary, spendingSummary, goalProgress and paymentProgress. Tests cover PKR/USD/JPY/KWD, custom cycles, absent versus zero plans, signed withdrawals, lifetime goal starting money and actuals unchanged by plans or attribution. No exchange conversion, bank balance, earned withdrawal income, investment prices or profits are implied. Income left is now shown even when no income is recorded instead of hiding a negative activity total. Its readable breakdown uses the same arithmetic.

Savings may be general or goal-linked; compatible existing category IDs remain intact, and new general savings uses/reuses a general classification without requiring another decision. Investment categories remain required. Payment type remains a fixed optional expense label in single and batch entry. Goals complete manually; withdrawing later does not automatically reopen one. Archive uses the existing reversible paused state and preserves records/older plans. Spending saved goal money remains two explicitly different recording actions; a combined withdrawal/expense workflow and automatic transfers are not claimed.

## Delivered behavior

- Every workspace header offers Record → Expense/Income/Savings/Investment. Contextual goal and receipt actions open the same form with relevant links/currency prefilled. Action-specific titles, visible date/currency, optional details, searchable recents/custom categories and sticky save footer.
- Savings requires an amount only; goal/purpose and legacy classification are separate. Contributions/withdrawals have plain labels. Shared amount validation rejects zero, negative, excess precision and invalid inputs. Draft cancellation is deliberate; failed saves keep inputs, stable IDs and retry. Save & add another retains useful defaults while clearing amount/note. Saving prevents repeated submission.
- Goal creation/editing optionally selects existing contributions and withdrawals with a net/lifetime preview and an explicit starting-money acknowledgement. Goal/plan/links commit together. Single attribution per record, conditional eligibility, no new actual events and no silent reassignment. Cards show saved/target/remaining/overfunded amount, lifetime versus selected-period progress and linked activity.
- Expected payments have an editable expected date and optional inline source creation. Existing compatible unmatched income can be selected and previewed; linking adds no income. Partial/overdue/cancelled/received states are distinct. Actual receipts may exceed the original expectation. Corrections/removals recalculate receipt progress.
- Removing an entry creates a private recovery snapshot and removes the active record in one transaction. Money Log → Removed entries restores original ID/date/amount/links under the normal validation rules. Records remain recoverable until restored or account deletion; no timed purge or Undo promise. Restore archived categories or reopen cancelled expectations first when necessary. A withdrawal/removed contribution cannot make goal progress negative. Recovery references protect category history; account deletion cascades recovery too.
- Visible Money Log type filters replace the redundant empty-filter scope line, keeping at least three records visible on a 390×660 phone; applied filter chips still name their scope. They retain the shared list/export predicate and current scope across corrections. Goal/source/expectation names are searchable and associations are visible. Exports preserve direction, category, goal and receipt attribution; removed records are excluded. Expected-payment export includes the due date.
- Optional recording diagnostics are off by default and remain browser-only: aggregate started/completed/failed/validation/correction/linking counts and coarse completion-time buckets. No content, amounts, identifiers, names, analytics SDK or network collector. Turning this off deletes the local counters. This creates a local baseline, not a claim of product-wide measurements or achievement of usability targets. Google Analytics work stays paused.

## Backend rollout and compatibility

Migrations015 and016 are additive/rerunnable. They add expected_date, a private RLS read-only recovery table, invoker atomic goal/receipt functions and narrowly scoped definer remove/restore functions. No frontend service-role key. Ordinary authenticated direct deletion is revoked, so the new UI must follow migration application; old clients receive an error on direct deletion during rollout rather than losing data. Existing planning validation, row ownership and recovery snapshot protection remain enforced. Sources with first expectations now preserve expected dates atomically. Migration016 protects recoverable source/payment classification and blocks stale create retries from resurrecting a removed ID; restore removes the protected snapshot and reinserts the original record in one transaction, with full rollback on any validation failure.
